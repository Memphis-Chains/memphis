Memphis Federation

The protocol that lets AI agents negotiate, delegate, and trust each other — without a central god.

The Problem

Today's AI agents are isolated silos. They can't talk to each other without human mediation. They can't delegate. They can't negotiate. They can't trust.

Vector search gives you retrieve. APIs give you data transfer. Federation gives you agents that actually work together.

Three-Layer Architecture

ML — Memphis Language

A DSL for humans and agents. Define agents, negotiate scope, express intent. Not just data — intentions.

MP — Memphis Protocol

Signed, timestamped, auditable. JSON-like but immutable once sent. Full chain of custody.

Rust Core + Chains

SHA-256 append-only chains. No modification, no deletion. Audit trail that survives courts.

How It Speaks

(request :from marcin :to synjar :action search :query "dokumenty projektu X" :deadline 2026-03-28 :trust-level high :scope [documents, metadata] :confidential true)

Federation in Practice

Connect Memphis to Synjar, Hermes, or any other agent runtime. Each agent declares its dialect, its capabilities, its trust requirements. They negotiate in real time.

No central authority. No god-mode cloud. Just agents talking to agents — auditable, signed, and immutable.

Why This Matters

Vector Search = you retrieve what you stored. RAG = you retrieve what someone else decided matters. Memphis Federation = agents that negotiate, delegate, and prove what they did.

This is the difference between a smart database and a sovereign AI agent.
