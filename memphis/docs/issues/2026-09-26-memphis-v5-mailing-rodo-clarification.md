### 6. What's the RODO consent text version we need?

The Polish RODO requires:
- (a) who is the controller (Memphis-Chains / Wodzu / Zawoja)
- (b) what data is collected (email, hashed IP, hashed UA)
- (c) purpose (newsletter, product updates, partnership opportunities)
- (d) legal basis (consent, Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR)
- (e) recipients (no third parties without consent)
- (f) retention period (until unsubscribe + 30 days for backup, then erased)
- (g) data subject rights (access, rectify, erase, restrict, portability, object)
- (h) how to exercise rights (email)
- (i) right to lodge complaint with PUODO

This text needs to be on a /rodo or /privacy page and shown in the signup form.
