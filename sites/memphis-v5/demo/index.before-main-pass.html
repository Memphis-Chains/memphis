Why Memphis exists

sovereignty, translated into runtime control

Memphis was born from Oswobodzeni, but its public face should speak in operational terms. Sovereignty here is not a slogan. It means control over infrastructure, data, secrets, boundaries, and the operator loop itself. In a world of rented AI, Memphis is the stack you can actually own.
Your machine, your memory, your vault, your operator loop.
